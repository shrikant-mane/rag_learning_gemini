TF-IDF stands for Term Frequency–Inverse Document Frequency.

TF-IDF is a numerical technique used to measure how important a word is to a document relative to a 
collection of documents.

TF-IDF assigns greater importance to words that are frequent in a particular document but relatively 
rare across the collection.

TF (Term Frequency)
    How frequently does the word occur in this document?


IDF (Inverse Document Frequency)
    How rare is the word across all documents?


TF-IDF(t,d)=TF(t,d) X IDF(t)


==============================
Term Frequency (TF)

TF(t,d) = (Count of term t in document d)/ (Total terms in documrnt d)


document = "python python aws java"
TF('python') = 2/4 => 0.5
TF('aws') = 1/4 => 0.25

=============================

Inverse Document Frequency (IDF)
IDF(t) = ln[(N+1) / (DF(t)+1)] + 1

N= total number of documents.
DF(t) = number of documents containing the term.
ln = natural logarithm.
